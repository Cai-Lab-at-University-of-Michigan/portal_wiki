*This tutorial explains how data is shared with other people on the portal.*

---

## Personal and group space

`Uploads` and `Library` each have two contexts, shown as buttons at the top of
the page:

- **personal** holds your own uploads and datasets.
- **group** holds the shared space of the group you belong to. Everyone in the
  group sees the same files and datasets.

Switch context before uploading if the data is meant to be shared. Files uploaded
to your personal space are not visible to the rest of the group.

Group membership is set by the site administrator. If the group button does not
appear, you have not been added to one.

---

## Sharing one dataset

In `Library`, open a dataset's `Actions` menu and click `Share Dataset`. The
dialog lists the people who can reach it, and you add or remove them by account.
There is no public link: everyone you share with needs a portal account.

---

## Sharing a scene

`Share Scene` creates a link to a saved view rather than to the data. The
recipient still needs an account, and receives their own copy of the scene. See
[Scenes](scenes.md).

---

### Troubleshooting

- **A colleague cannot see my upload**: it went to personal space. Upload it to
  the group folder, or ask the administrator to move it.
- **The group folder is empty in Globus**: switch to the group context in the
  portal before clicking `Upload Files via Globus`.
