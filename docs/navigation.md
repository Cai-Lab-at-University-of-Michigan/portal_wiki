## Sidebar

The sidebar is a narrow strip of icons. Hover over it to open it and show the names. On a narrow window, `Open Menu` replaces the strip. `Keep open` at the bottom of the open sidebar pins it in place. To always show it open, choose `Classic panel` under `Settings`, then `Appearance`.

The `Pages` group holds:

| Entry | Use it to |
|---|---|
| `Dashboard` | Land here after login. It only greets you. |
| `Uploads` | See the files you transferred and convert them. See [Uploads](file-uploads.md). |
| `Library` | Open, share and manage converted datasets. See [Library](image-library-management.md). |
| `Scenes` | Manage saved views. See [Scenes](scenes.md). |
| `Jobs` | Follow background work. See [Jobs](jobs.md). |
| `Settings` | Change your profile, password and appearance. |

Site administrators also see `Admin`.

The `Plugins` group includes these tools, which work on the scene open in the viewer. Some sites add more entries.

| Entry | Page |
|---|---|
| `Interactive Annotation` | [Interactive Annotation](interactive-annotation.md) |
| `Online Transform` | [Online Transform](online-transform.md) |
| `Virtual Stitching` | [Virtual Stitching](virtual-stitching.md) |
| `Spot Detection` | [Spot Detection](spot-detection.md) |
| `Motion Correction` | [Motion Correction](motion-correction.md) |

Open a dataset or scene first (see [Library](image-library-management.md)). Without one, a plugin page says `Please open a scene to use <plugin name>`. Keep the viewer window open while you use a plugin.

## Top bar

At the top right, the sun or moon icon switches between light and dark, and your name opens a menu with `My Profile` (goes to `Settings`) and `Log Out`. On sites that have documentation, the document icon opens it.

---

## Settings

Open `Settings` in the sidebar, or `My Profile` in the account menu. The `User Settings` page has these tabs:

- `My profile` shows your `Full name` and `Email`. `Edit` changes them. `Organization/Group` is set by a site administrator.
- `Security` changes your password: enter `Current Password`, `New Password` and `Confirm Password`, then click `Save`.
- `Appearance` holds `Theme`, `Sidebar` (`Compact rail` or `Classic panel`), `Row height`, `Columns` (which columns each list shows) and `Reset all appearance settings`. The choices are stored on your account.
- `Danger zone` has a `Delete` button that permanently deletes your account after you confirm. Site administrators do not see this tab. Ask the site administrator what happens to stored files.

## Lists

`Uploads`, `Library`, `Scenes` and `Jobs` use the same table.

- Click a column title to search the text in that column. Press `Esc` to clear the search. The `Type` column in `Library` opens a list of types to tick instead.
- Hover a column title to show a sort arrow, then click it to change the order. A few columns, such as `Type` and `Actions`, have none.
- The `Actions` column holds a three-dot menu. This guide calls it the row menu.
- `Select` switches a list to bulk mode, where you tick rows and act on them together.
- Use `Show` and the page controls under the table for long lists.
- Drag the edge between two column titles to resize a column.
- Choose which columns appear under `Settings`, `Appearance`, `Columns`.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **A table is wider than the window**: scroll it sideways, or hide columns under `Settings`, `Appearance`, `Columns`.
